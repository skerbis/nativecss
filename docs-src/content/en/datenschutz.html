No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and embeds no analytics, advertising or social media services. Fonts (no Google Fonts), icons, images and videos are on the same server as the website.

Settings stored locally

When you change something under "Display" (colour scheme, contrast, motion, text size, font …), your browser stores that choice in local storage (localStorage, entries ncss-theme and ncss-prefs) so it is kept for your next visit. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to anyone. Storing it is strictly necessary for the function you explicitly asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). To delete it: "Reset" in the panel, or clear the site data in your browser.

Maps (only after a click)

Examples with maps load nothing from third parties at first. Only when you click "Show map" are the map style and tiles loaded from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org); your IP address is transmitted to that service. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. The legal basis is your consent by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); it applies to that page view only.

External links

Links to GitHub, Pexels and other websites are only requested when you click them. From then on, that provider's privacy policy applies.

Contact by email

If you email me, I use your details only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed for that. Contributions on GitHub (issues, pull requests, sponsoring) are subject to GitHub's terms.
