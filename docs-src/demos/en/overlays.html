Native elements (details, dialog)

Expand details

Content shown/hidden by the native <details> element - no JS needed. The "box" look now comes explicitly from .ncss-disclosure, no longer from the bare tag.

Native <dialog> as .ncss-modal

Focus trap, ESC to close, ::backdrop, fade+scale animation - all from the browser/CSS, no JS modal framework.

3D variants: perspective() as a transform function in the element's own transform chain (no perspective on a parent element needed or effective, since <dialog> renders in the top layer).

.ncss-modal--3d

Tilts forward out of the depth when opening, instead of just fading/scaling.

.ncss-modal--3d-flip

Spins in once around its vertical axis when opening, like a card.

.ncss-modal--3d-swing

Swings in like a door on a hinge at the left edge.

Entrance variants: instead of the default scaling, the modal slides/grows in from one direction (a slower, strongly decelerating Bézier curve instead of the neutral default curve).

.ncss-modal--slide-up

Slides in from the bottom when opening.

.ncss-modal--slide-down

Slides in from the top when opening.

.ncss-modal--slide-left

Slides in from the right when opening.

.ncss-modal--slide-right

Slides in from the left when opening.

.ncss-modal--zoom

Grows out of the center more noticeably than the default fade when opening.

Off-canvas content
