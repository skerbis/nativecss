Custom players as web components on top of native <video>/<audio>, scroll-controlled video, background video with a pause toggle, video inside text, mini player and preview cards. Each component is its own CSS file (+ at most one script) and can be used individually - without JS, the native element stays usable everywhere.

All clips and sounds on this page are self-made (canvas animation or synthesis) - free of licensing concerns.

Video and audio player

<ncss-video-player> and <ncss-audio-player> from a single mold: the same icons, a round play button in the accent color, the same timeline with chapter gaps, the same chapter and playlist lists. In the video player the controls sit on a soft gradient over the image; speed and caption language live in the gear menu. Keys in the focused player: Space/K, J/L, arrows, M, F, C, N/P, 0-9. Only one player plays at a time.

Scroll video <ncss-scroll-video>

Scrolling scrubs the video forward and back - a day over the hills, from dawn to night. The stage sticks edge to edge in the viewport (object-fit: cover, no bars). With reduced motion: a regular video with controls, texts below it.

Noon: clear air, the clouds drift on.

The sunset bathes the ridges in warm light.

Night: the moon rises, the stars appear.

Image sequence <ncss-scroll-frames>

The same scene as 72 individual WebP frames on a canvas - scrubs smoothly in every engine because nothing has to be decoded or seeked. Loading only starts near the viewport, coarse first (every 16th frame), then refined.

Scrollytelling + video

An <ncss-scroll-video> inside an <ncss-scrolly> follows the steps: data-ncss-time="2-4" scrubs from 2 to 4 s while the step crosses the middle of the screen. With reduced motion, the video jumps per step (event ncss-scrolly-step) instead of scrubbing.

Background video .ncss-video-bg

For any surface: a tint for readable text (light/dark/brand/gradient), a visible pause toggle, loads only near the viewport, pauses when out of view and in background tabs. With reduced motion or data saver mode nothing is loaded - only the poster.

Calm background, clear text

The dark tint sets color-scheme: dark - text, buttons and cards inside the surface automatically resolve their colors to the dark half, in both the light and the dark page theme.

Hero with video

The same script on a .ncss-hero: data-ncss-video-bg is all it takes - the video in .ncss-hero-media gets a pause toggle, lazy loading and motion sensitivity.

Video in text .ncss-video-text

The heading stays real text; a single blend mode (hard-light) shows the video inside the letters - the surrounding surface follows the page color in light and dark.

Mini player <ncss-video-float>

Start the video and keep scrolling: the playing player docks at the bottom right and returns to its place when you scroll back. "Close" pauses it.

Preview cards .ncss-video-card

Hover with the mouse or focus with Tab - the card plays a muted preview. Not a single byte of video is loaded beforehand (preload="none"); on touch devices and with reduced motion, the poster stays.

Encoding video for scrubbing

Scrubbing means seeking - and a jump can only start decoding at a keyframe. Regular web videos have a keyframe every 2-10 s: Chrome glosses over this, Safari stutters visibly. So for scroll video, make every frame a keyframe (-g 1, all-intra) or use a very short GOP. Covered in detail in the docs.

# Scroll video: H.264, every frame a keyframe, no audio, metadata up front
ffmpeg -i quelle.mov -an -vf "scale=1280:-2,fps=24" \
  -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 30 -g 1 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -movflags +faststart scrub.mp4
