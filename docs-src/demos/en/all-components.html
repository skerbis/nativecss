Full-bleed section

Several variants to make clear what .ncss-full-bleed does exactly - the trick always breaks out to 100vw, no matter how wide or narrow the surrounding container currently is.

1. Edge-to-edge background, content stays at normal width (the most common case)

This <section> stays structurally nested in <main class="ncss-container">, but visually breaks out edge to edge (.ncss-full-bleed) - the inner .ncss-container keeps the text at normal column width again, only the background extends to the screen edge.

2. Without an inner .ncss-container: the content breaks out too

Without the inner .ncss-container, the TEXT also runs to the screen edge - usually only sensible for a single edge-to-edge image, video or a plain color band, not for body text.

3. Also breaks out of a narrow parent container

This block sits inside a narrow .ncss-container--narrow - the full-bleed trick still breaks out to the real screen edge, regardless of how tight its direct parent frame currently is.

4. One-sided ("bookmark"): .ncss-bleed-end only breaks out to the right

On the left this quote stays normally in the text column, on the right it breaks out to the screen edge - like a bookmark sticking out of the content. The mirrored variant .ncss-bleed-start breaks out to the left instead.

5. From the middle to the right: .ncss-bleed-half-end - the START edge (left) sits exactly at the middle of the parent element, the END edge (right) breaks out to the screen edge

Starts in the middle, breaks out to the right up to the screen edge.

6. From the middle to the left: .ncss-bleed-half-start - mirror image of no. 5

Breaks out to the left up to the screen edge, ends in the middle.

7. Symmetrically from the middle: .ncss-bleed-center grows equally on both sides by a fixed amount but stays centered on the middle of the parent element (a different mechanism, not tied to the viewport - so it also works in a non-centered container)

Grows 1.5rem beyond the card on each side, but stays centered.
