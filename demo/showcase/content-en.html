Everything that gets read on a page: type, colours, surfaces, cards, lists, tables and the small pieces in between – with content from Hotel Seeblick, the municipality of Seeburg and ImmoDesk. Use the Inspect button bottom right (or Alt+I), click a block and see which classes build it.

Fluid type scale & base elements

Six size steps, each a clamp() between a minimum and a maximum – type grows smoothly with the window, without a single breakpoint. Headings, paragraphs, quotes and inline elements are styled without any class.

Tip: Make the window wider and narrower – the samples change size fluidly.

  • --ncss-font-size-sm … 2xl
  • .ncss-text-sm/base/md/lg/xl
  • h1–h6, p, blockquote

Drop cap: sizes & weight

Small (--sm): two to three lines tall, calm enough for a sidebar or a newsletter teaser with little text.

Large and light (--lg --light): four to five lines, regular weight instead of bold – elegant for features and long intros.

Text on photos: light, dark, boxed

The theme cannot help on a photo – an image stays light or dark whether the page is in dark mode or not. These classes fix the text to white or black, in four opacity steps. .ncss-text-boxed puts a highlighter box behind every single line.

Watch out: .ncss-text-boxed needs display: inline – do not use it as a direct child of a stack or grid, wrap it in its own element.

  • .ncss-text-light
  • .ncss-text-light-100/-300/-700/-900
  • .ncss-text-dark
  • .ncss-text-dark-100/-300/-700/-900
  • .ncss-text-boxed

Colour tokens & graded scales

Every token carries its light and dark value via light-dark() – no second stylesheet. From exactly one base colour per family, color-mix() derives steps 100 to 900 and a matching text colour -on-soft.

Tip: switch the theme top right – every swatch follows, and 100 and 300 stay subtle in the dark too.

Coloured surfaces, local colour scheme & gradients

One class sets the background and guaranteed readable text together – no hand-tuned contrast. .ncss-scheme-dark/-light switch a whole subtree to the other colour scheme, including every card and badge inside. Two curated gradients, angle via custom property.

Watch out: use gradient text only for large headings – in body text the contrast varies from letter to letter.

Code block with copy button

A plain <pre><code class="language-html"> – code-block.js highlights it with self-hosted Prism.js and adds a copy button. The colours come from the same tokens as buttons and badges.

Cards: structure, image & orientation

.ncss-card is structure only – a column with image, header, body and footer. Borders, corners and shadows come from the utilities above. The image sits on top or at the bottom depending on markup order; it moves next to the text as soon as the card itself is at least 24 rem wide.

Watch out: the horizontal card needs the .ncss-card-container wrapper – an element cannot query its own width with a container query.

Watch out: in the grid every card needs exactly as many direct children as --ncss-card-grid-rows (default 3: image, body, footer).

Tables on narrow screens

Two strategies: scroll sideways (keeps full table semantics – the default recommendation) or stack into record cards below 36 rem container width. Plus a compact variant for dense data.

Watch out: stacking needs data-label on every cell and sacrifices row/column announcements in screen readers – only use it for genuine “records”.
