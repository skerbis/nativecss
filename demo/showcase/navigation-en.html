Top bar with dropdown, submenu and mega menu

The top bar arranges brand, menu and actions. Submenus are <details>: they open by click, tap or keyboard – hover plays no part. Below a 64 rem window width the same menu becomes an off-canvas panel behind the ☰ button, without duplicate markup.

Note: A menu item with a submenu is only a toggle, not a page of its own. So the overview page goes into the submenu as its first entry (here “All rooms”).

Many long items: priority navigation

<ncss-nav-priority> moves everything that no longer fits on the line into a “More” menu – measured by its own space, not the window. The chapter bar at the very top of this page works the same way.

Note: Opt-in – load components/nav-priority.css and js/nav-priority.js separately. Without JavaScript the list simply wraps onto several lines.

Slide drilldown in the mobile menu

Instead of expanding submenus inside the off-canvas panel, <ncss-nav-drilldown> slides the whole panel on to the next level – with a “Back” at the top, like the settings on a phone. The markup stays the normal dropdown.

Note: Only takes effect in the mobile menu (below a 64 rem window width). That is why the example sits in a narrow frame with its own viewport.

  • <ncss-nav-drilldown>
  • back-text
  • .ncss-nav-drill-back
In the handbook: slide drilldown →
  1. Tap ☰ in the frame – the menu slides in from the left.
  2. Tap “Rooms & suites” or “Wellness”: the panel glides to the sub-level, with “Back – Wellness” at the top.
  3. “Back – Wellness” returns to the first level; closing resets the menu.

Section navigation “On this page”

The slim bar below the header jumps to the sections of long pages and highlights the section in view. On phones you swipe it sideways. Top bar and section bar share one sticky container so they never slip apart.

Note: The bar belongs right after the top bar, not inside <main> – otherwise it only sticks within its own section.

  • .ncss-subnav-inner
  • .ncss-subnav-label
  • .ncss-subnav-list
  • data-ncss-subnav
  • data-ncss-sticky-header

Breadcrumb

The path to the current page as an ordered list. The last step is not a link but carries aria-current="page".

Note: The aria-label on the <nav> is required – otherwise several navigations on one page cannot be told apart.

Pagination

Real links for paging through archives and lists – shareable, back-button friendly, no JavaScript. When the bar itself gets narrower than 32 rem, only the first, current and last page plus arrows remain.

Note: On the first page keep the previous link without href (plus aria-disabled="true") – so the bar does not jump while paging.

Tabs

<ncss-tabs> turns jump links above sections into real tabs following the WAI-ARIA pattern: arrow keys switch, Home/End jump to the ends. Without JavaScript the sections simply stack.

Tip: With activation="manual" the arrows only move focus and Enter switches – good for panels with heavy content.

  • <ncss-tabs>
  • .ncss-tabs-list
  • .ncss-tabs-panel
  • .ncss-tabs-heading
  • .ncss-tabs--vertical
  • activation="manual"

Search: growing field and live search

The search field starts as a magnifier and widens on focus – pure CSS. Live search shows grouped results while you type, fully keyboard-operable; without JavaScript it stays a normal search form.

Tip: data-mode="client" loads a static JSON file once and filters in the browser – ideal for small sites without a search server.

  • .ncss-search-input
  • .ncss-livesearch
  • .ncss-livesearch-results
  • data-ncss-livesearch

Hide a bar on scroll

A sticky bar disappears when scrolling down and returns when scrolling up – pure CSS via a scroll-state container query. Here in its own scroll area, so this page’s header stays put.

Note: Chromium only so far; elsewhere the bar simply stays visible. For whole-page scrolling, js/hide-on-scroll-fallback.js closes the gap.

  • .ncss-hide-on-scroll
  • .ncss-sticky
  • container-type: scroll-state

Weekly market moves

From the first Saturday in December the weekly market takes place on Church Square. The market square is being repaved until the end of February; the bus stop moves to Seestraße during that time.

Advent market by the lake

Every weekend from 28 November to 21 December, from 2 pm. Proceeds from the fire brigade’s mulled-wine stall go to the clubs’ youth work.

Bulky waste in December

The collection on the fourth Thursday is cancelled because of the holidays and moved to Monday, 29 December. Please register by 19 December at the citizens’ office.

Bathing jetty closed in winter

The bathing jetty is closed from December to March for safety reasons. Ice bathing is only allowed during the water rescue service’s registered sessions.

Town council

The next public meeting is on Thursday, 11 December, at 7 pm in the town hall’s main chamber. The agenda is posted in the notice case a week before.

“Back to top” link

A plain link to #top. Where the browser supports scroll-driven animations, it floats as a round button at the bottom right and only appears after some scrolling – on this page it has been there all along, just above the Inspect button. Otherwise it stays right here in the text.

Note: Put it at the end of the page – that is also the right spot for the fallback. --ncss-back-to-top-offset lifts it when the corner is already taken.
