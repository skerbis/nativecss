Modern UI

The building blocks that make a page feel “modern” today – bento grids, spotlights, before/after sliders, toasts, soft gradients, glass and glow. Each is its own file, and without JavaScript there is always a sensible base state. Use the Inspect button at the bottom right (or Alt+I) and click a block to see its classes and markup.

Bento templates

You know the base grid .ncss-bento from the Layout chapter. The templates assign spans by position – not a single class on the tiles, ideal for feature overviews and photo galleries.

Note: grid-auto-flow: dense only reorders visually – tab order still follows the source.

  • .ncss-bento--hero
  • .ncss-bento--mosaic
  • --ncss-bento-row-height

Spotlight

A soft cone of light follows the mouse pointer, and the edge nearest the pointer lights up. data-ncss-spotlight on the group is enough: a single listener, and neighbouring cards catch the edge of the light too.

Good to know: touch is ignored; without JavaScript and on keyboard focus the light sits in the centre.

  • .ncss-spotlight
  • .ncss-spotlight--strong
  • data-ncss-spotlight
  • --ncss-spotlight-color
  • js/spotlight.js

Before/after comparison

<ncss-compare> lays a real range slider over two images – mouse, finger, arrow keys and screen readers all work because the browser provides them. Great for renovations, photo edits or day/night.

Note: without JavaScript you get a static 50/50 split, and the slider is deliberately hidden.

  • <ncss-compare>
  • .ncss-compare-before
  • .ncss-compare-after
  • .ncss-compare-range
  • --ncss-compare-ratio

Expanding panels

Pure CSS: the panel under the pointer or focus grows and reveals its text, otherwise the active one does. In narrow containers the panels stack and grow in height.

Tip: panels are links or buttons – with no target URL use buttons, so a tap on a phone expands them.

  • .ncss-expand-panels
  • .ncss-expand-panel
  • .ncss-expand-panel--active
  • --ncss-expand-height
In the handbook: Expanding panels →

Notifications

Toasts via ncss.notify(), or with no script of your own via data-ncss-notify on a button. Timers pause on pointer, focus or a background tab; errors stay until dismissed.

Good to know: the region lives in the top layer – toasts appear above an open modal too, without any z-index.

  • ncss.notify()
  • data-ncss-notify
  • <ncss-toast-region>
  • .ncss-notification
  • js/notification.js

Aurora & Mesh

Soft, slowly drifting fields of brand colour behind a section – just radial gradients and transform, no expensive blur filter. Text stays readable in light and dark.

Note: use --vivid only behind large headings and short texts.

  • .ncss-aurora
  • .ncss-aurora--vivid
  • .ncss-mesh
  • .ncss-mesh--static
  • --ncss-aurora-speed

Effects: glow, glass, stamp, grain

Five purely decorative classes to combine – on buttons, cards or any surface, with no extra markup. Tunable per element via custom properties.

Note: .ncss-glass creates a new containing block – don’t put it directly on an element that contains dropdowns or popovers.

  • .ncss-glow-border
  • .ncss-glow-pulse
  • .ncss-glass
  • .ncss-stamped
  • .ncss-stamped--press
  • .ncss-grain
