Media

Crop images, put text on photos, page through galleries, play video and sound – from plain CSS helpers to full custom players. All media load only when needed. Use the Inspect button at the bottom right (or Alt+I) and click a block to see the classes behind it.

Image helpers

Small classes for what nearly every image needs: a fixed aspect ratio, cropping or fitting, rounded corners or a circle. The focus point decides which part stays visible when cropping.

Watch out: .ncss-img-dim-in-dark follows the system setting (prefers-color-scheme), not the theme toggle above.

  • .ncss-ratio-video
  • .ncss-ratio-square
  • .ncss-ratio-portrait
  • --ncss-focus-point
  • .ncss-img-cover
  • .ncss-img-contain
  • .ncss-img-rounded
  • .ncss-img-circle
  • .ncss-img-dim-in-dark

Image in running text

For a single image inside running text, e.g. from a CMS editor: the editor picks width and alignment, the text wraps around it.

Watch out: The widths deliberately do not shrink automatically – on phones prefer --width-full or --align-center.

  • .ncss-figure
  • --width-25/-50/-75/-full
  • --align-left/-right/-center

Breakfast with a lake view

At Hotel Seeblick the day starts on the terrace: fresh bread from the village bakery, honey from the beekeeper down the road and eggs from the farms around the lake. Get up early and you will still see the mist lying on the water.

When it rains we set the tables in the conservatory. The buffet is open from seven to eleven, an hour longer at weekends – late risers can have breakfast brought to their room.

After breakfast the spa is worth a visit: the Finnish sauna is warm from ten, and the relaxation room with its mountain view stays open all day. Bathrobes are waiting in your room.

Prefer to get outside? We rent rowing boats at our own jetty – a lap around the little island takes about an hour.

Art direction with <picture>

A different image per screen width – not just a different crop, but a different file. HTML does this on its own; NativeCSS needs no class for it. Make the window narrower than 40rem: the panorama turns into a portrait shot.

Watch out: There is no cross-browser equivalent for <video> – there only a different crop of the same file helps (see Video).

  • <picture>
  • <source media srcset>

Overlay on an image

A panel on top of a photo: a caption along an edge, a tint over the whole area or a centred button. A gradient (--scrim) or an even tint (--tint) keeps text readable on any subject.

Tip: With .ncss-reveal-on-hover an overlay only appears on hover or keyboard focus – on touch devices it is always visible.

  • .ncss-overlay-container
  • .ncss-overlay
  • --top
  • --bottom
  • --center
  • --scrim
  • --tint
  • .ncss-overlay-container--zoom
  • .ncss-reveal-on-hover
  • .ncss-text-light

Hero section

A large photo with content on top – the classic page intro. The text sits either directly on the image (with a gradient) or on an opaque card. Parallax makes the image move slower while scrolling, without any JavaScript.

Watch out: Never pass a background image through a custom property – relative url()s then resolve against the CSS file. An <img alt=""> inside .ncss-hero-media avoids that.

  • .ncss-hero
  • .ncss-hero-media
  • .ncss-hero-content
  • --transparent
  • --filled
  • .ncss-hero--parallax

Cover for embeds

An <iframe> (video or map embed) fills its frame edge to edge, however wide or tall it is – overflowing edges are clipped, nothing is distorted. For <img> and <video>, .ncss-img-cover is enough.

Watch out: The container needs its own height from outside. A local 16:9 still is embedded here – a YouTube embed behaves the same.

  • .ncss-cover-container
  • .ncss-cover

Slideshow

Swipeable images via native scroll snapping: touch, trackpad, mouse wheel and arrow keys work without a line of JavaScript. Recent Chromium versions add dots and arrows via CSS.

Tip: --peek lets the next slide peek in – a clear hint that there is more.

  • .ncss-slideshow
  • .ncss-slideshow-track
  • .ncss-slideshow-item
  • .ncss-slideshow-item--peek
  • --ncss-slideshow-item-width
  • .ncss-slideshow-track--no-scrollbar

Lightbox gallery

Click an image: it opens large in a native dialog; arrow keys, swiping and previous/next page through all links of the same group. Without JavaScript the link simply opens the large image.

Tip: The caption comes from data-caption, otherwise from the thumbnail’s alt text.

  • data-ncss-lightbox="group"
  • data-caption
  • .ncss-lightbox
  • js/lightbox.js

Map

<ncss-map> shows an interactive vector map with MapLibre GL (self-hosted). In two-click mode (consent) nothing is loaded from the map server until someone clicks “Show map” – so nothing comes from OpenFreeMap before the click here.

Good to know: the content inside the element is the no-JavaScript fallback and stays available to screen readers. On your own site, proxy routes every request through your own server, and then the click is no longer needed.

  • <ncss-map>
  • lat / lng / zoom
  • label
  • consent
  • proxy
  • --ncss-map-height

Video

A <video> with poster and controls already is a complete click-to-play player. .ncss-video adds a fixed aspect ratio and a caption, just like for images.

Tip: --responsive-crop crops the same video to portrait on narrow screens – make the window narrower.

  • <video poster controls>
  • .ncss-video
  • .ncss-ratio-video
  • .ncss-video--responsive-crop

Video player

A custom player on top of the native <video>: timeline with chapters, captions, speed, picture-in-picture, fullscreen and full keyboard control (Space, J/L, arrows, M, F, C). As a card with chapter list and playlist.

Good to know: Only one player plays at a time. Without JavaScript the native video stays fully usable.

  • <ncss-video-player>
  • layout="card"
  • .ncss-video-player-playlist
  • data-chapters
  • data-captions
  • --ncss-video-player-accent

Audio

Two levels: .ncss-audio only gives the native element a consistent width (optionally in a titled frame); the audio player adds a waveform, chapters, skip buttons, speed and a playlist – in the same look as the video player.

Good to know: The waveform decodes the file once in the browser – this only works for same-origin files (up to 8 MB).

  • .ncss-audio
  • .ncss-audio-card
  • <ncss-audio-player>
  • layout="card"
  • waveform
  • .ncss-audio-player-playlist

Background video

A muted loop behind any content – band, card or hero. The tint keeps the text readable and switches the colours inside to match. The script adds a visible pause button, loads only near the viewport and pauses when out of sight.

Good to know: With reduced motion or data saver nothing is loaded – only the poster, and the button offers “Play”.

  • .ncss-video-bg
  • --dark
  • --light
  • --brand
  • --gradient
  • .ncss-video-bg-media
  • data-ncss-video-bg
  • --ncss-video-bg-strength

Video in text

Mini player

Start the video and keep scrolling: the playing player docks to the bottom right and returns to its place when you scroll back. “Close” or Esc pauses; focus is never moved.

Good to know: Only a playing video docks – a paused one simply scrolls away.

  • <ncss-video-float>
  • .ncss-video-float-frame
  • position="start"
In the handbook: Mini player →

Scroll video

Scrolling scrubs a video forwards and backwards – a day over the hills, from dawn to night. The stage sticks edge to edge in view, texts appear at set times.

Watch out: Encode every frame as a keyframe for scrubbing (-g 1), otherwise Safari stutters. With reduced motion or data saver: a normal video with the texts below.

  • <ncss-scroll-video>
  • length
  • .ncss-scroll-video-stage
  • .ncss-scroll-video-caption
  • data-ncss-time="1-3"
  • --ncss-scroll-video-top
